# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a place to collaborate on spend-integrity software. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback about the work, and keep it specific.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's credentials, bills, or personal data.

Harassment, slurs, doxxing, and sexual attention that is not welcome are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, contact the maintainer through GitHub (a private security advisory or a direct message). Reports are handled in confidence as far as the platform allows.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block people who break this standard.

## Scope

This applies to the repository and to spaces that represent this project, such as issue discussions and pull requests.
